## 2. Episode Caching & Frequency Calculations
- **Fixed Timezone Parsing**: Updated `feed_cache.go` (`parseFeedDate`) to map standard US timezone abbreviations (PDT, PST, EDT, EST, CDT, CST, MDT, MST) to explicit UTC offsets before `time.Parse`. This prevents the parser from defaulting unknown timezones to `UTC (+0000)`, which previously resulted in up to 8 hours of drift and ruined the accuracy of frequency tracking and the 24h queue policy calculations.
- **Added Coverage**: Added test case in `feed_cache_test.go` to explicitly verify `PDT` parsing.
